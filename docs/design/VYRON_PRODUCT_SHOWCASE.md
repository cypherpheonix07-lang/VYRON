## 1. Multi-Palette Storytelling Chapters

Rather than flattening the marketing and product storytelling into a generic single color, the VYRON product showcase progresses through seven thematic chapters mapped to the six multi-palette identities:

### Chapter 1 — Introduction & Vision
- **Theme**: **Midnight** (`#0A1A2F` / `#1F1F1F`)
- **Foundational Message**:
  - Autonomous Engineering Intelligence SaaS for complex system architectures, AST verification, and multi-tenant delivery governance.
  - Zero fabricated API metrics; grounded telemetry.

### Chapter 2 — Deep Engineering Capabilities
- **Theme**: **Midnight with Ocean Blue accents** (`#00D4FF`)
- **Foundational Message**:
  - 12-Stage AST Static Analysis & Cyclomatic Complexity.
  - Lizard AST metric extraction, Halstead volume, maintainability index.
  - Traceability from requirement to test execution.

### Chapter 3 — Cognitive AI & Agent Operations
- **Theme**: **Velvet / Obsidian Glow** (`#3A0CA3` / `#6A0DAD`)
- **Foundational Message**:
  - ATHER Cognitive Center & ATLAS System Knowledge Graph.
  - Epistemic promotion guards preventing unverified hallucinations from reaching production branches.
  - Specialized autonomous agents with bounded capability scopes.

### Chapter 4 — Collaboration & Developer Velocity
- **Theme**: **Pastel Breeze / Emerald Dark** (`#040404` / `#00C896`)
- **Foundational Message**:
  - WorkPulse velocity telemetry and developer flow state tracking.
  - Team review spaces with cryptographic evidence linking.
  - High-contrast Emerald buttons with `#040404` text ensuring WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility.

### Chapter 5 — Knowledge & Documentation
- **Theme**: **Warm Breeze** (`#EACEAA` Cream / `#D39858` Caramel / `#85431E` Rich Brown / `#34150F` Espresso)
- **Foundational Message**:
  - Approachable SRS traceability matrices, living documentation, and engineering architecture diagrams.
  - Human-centered product guides with high-contrast Espresso typography on warm surfaces.

### Chapter 6 — Deployment, Cloud & Edge Infrastructure
- **Theme**: **Ocean Blue / Neon Dusk** (`#020024` / `#00D4FF`)
- **Foundational Message**:
  - Multi-region edge runtime powered by Cloudflare Nitro workers.
  - Celery Flow & Sentinel continuous deployment pipelines.
  - 25-Gate automated promotion boundaries.

### Chapter 7 — Call to Action & Workspace Entry
- **Theme**: **Midnight Navy** (`#0A1A2F` / `#22D3EE` Focus)
- **Foundational Message**:
  - Dominant CTA: "Launch Engineering Workspace" (links to `/register` or `/login`).
  - Secondary CTA: "Explore Public Demonstration" (links to `/app/preview`).
